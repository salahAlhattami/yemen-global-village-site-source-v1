01

Culture

A Shared Language Among Nations

Yemen carries a civilizational and cultural legacy spanning thousands of years — a meeting point for civilizations, a station along the ancient trade routes, and a homeland of deep-rooted traditions in hospitality, arts, crafts, and architecture.

In a world drawn closer together through culture, knowledge, and shared human experience, there is a growing need for initiatives that open new horizons for dialogue, strengthen understanding between communities, and introduce national identities through living experiences that go beyond conventional forms of display.

From this vision, Yemeni Village was born as an international destination for cultural and civilizational exchange — taking visitors beyond observation into interaction, and from learning about cultural heritage to experiencing its details firsthand.

Hospitality The Arts Crafts Architecture Authentic Products Living Heritage

Yemen — a meeting point of civilizations, a station on the ancient world trade routes, and a homeland of deep-rooted traditions that still carry the identity of the land and the spirit of its people.

02

About Yemeni Village

A Destination Alive with Experience

The Village is built on the idea of creating a living destination that embraces the arts, heritage, hospitality, crafts, and authentic products within an interactive environment blending originality with the spirit of the present — a continuous experience renewed with every visit.

A Complete Yemeni Experience

It begins the moment visitors arrive — where culture meets hospitality, arts meet crafts, and heritage becomes a living experience through direct interaction, not passive observation.

Sustainable Bridges

Genuine connection between Yemen and the peoples of the world through culture, the arts, hospitality, and authentic products — partnerships whose impact extends beyond the visit.

A Flexible Model

It adapts to the nature and vision of each host country — within international exhibitions, cultural seasons, or as a permanent cultural destination operating year-round.

07

Flexible Solutions

A Format for Every Destination

Yemeni Village is designed as a flexible model that adapts the experience to the nature of each country — its cultural vision, spaces, and annual programs. It can be developed in several formats:

01

Permanent Cultural Destination

Operating year-round as a sustainable cultural landmark.

02

Seasonal Village

Within the host country's national seasons and events.

03

International Participation

Within international exhibitions and world Expos.

04

Companion Experience

For major festivals and events, as added cultural value.

05

Cultural Space

Within commercial complexes or tourism destinations.

06

Mobile Model

Implemented in more than one city within the same country.

This flexibility gives host entities the freedom to choose the model best suited to them, with the option to develop and expand it in the future to achieve cultural, tourism, community, and economic goals.

Impact Beyond Place

Empowering Crafts and Creative Industries

Dialogue Between Peoples

A Partnership That Creates Impact

Each experience is developed in partnership with the relevant entities — taking into account the location, cultural programs, and national seasons — so that Yemeni Village becomes an extension of the local cultural landscape, not a separate element within it.
